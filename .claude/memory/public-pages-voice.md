The pages meant for players (`doc/monster-behaviour.html` and its Markdown twin, and the
pages like it) describe **the classic Atari game**, as ReDMCSB has it. CSBWin and CSBweb
come in only where they differ, in a short note. The project is "CSBweb" there. "The
port" means something only inside the repository.

They read as a study, not a journal. Nothing like "I had expected", "Unsolved" and "Solved"
sections, or "I first called it wrong". An oddity of the code goes in a "Coding quirks"
section, sorted into actual bugs, strange but harmless, and still a mystery.

**Why:** the author asked for it (2026-11-09) after the behaviour page was rewritten
from ChristopheF's forum review and read as the history of its own corrections.

**How to apply:** before editing such a page, grep it for "the port", "I ", "unsolved"
and "solved". State the Atari's behaviour first, with its numbers. Credit a source as a
source, not as an event in the page's history. See [[text-changes-no-tests]].

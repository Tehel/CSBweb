Dungeon Master on the Atari ST, version by version

Six original disks, from the first release in December 1987 to the French translation of May 1988. Leaving the translations aside, this is what changed in the dungeon and in the game's tables, and why CSBWin's Dungeon Master is a mix of two releases.

Read from six original Atari ST disk images · dungeon.dat compared square by square · graphics.dat compared item by item

The short version: 1.0 was patched three days after release, and 1.1 is the one real rebalance. Attacks became more tiring, the Dragon attacks from further away, the Lock Picks are gone, and a skeleton ambush appeared on level 6. After that, 1.2 changed four bits and three words, and the translations changed only the language.

What never changes

All six disks use the same copy protection, the same FTL logo and the same launcher. Each one has its own packed game program. The dungeon's shape stays the same: 14 levels, the same start, the same walls almost everywhere. Levels are numbered here as the game numbers them, from 1, the Hall of Champions; the files number them from 0.

1.0, three days later8 December 1987 → 11 December 1987

Both disks are labelled 1.0, but the second one fixes a handful of things the first got wrong.

The crack switch stays on the wall

On level 5, a crack in the wall hides a button. On the first disk, pressing it swaps the picture for one drawn at eye level, so the crack jumps up the wall, and the "pressed" crack is empty. The second disk draws the button pushed into the crack and keeps it low, where the crack was.

Fury gets a blade

The picture of Fury lying on the floor was a plain brown shaft. The second disk gives it a grey blade. On both 1.0 disks the Staff of Manar shares Fury's picture, so the staff now looks like a sword on the floor. 1.1 fixes that.

In the dungeon

  • Nabi comes with clothes. On the first disk Nabi's mirror holds nothing. The second gives Nabi a Staff, a Tunic, Blue Pants and Sandals.
  • Level 2's doors are redone. Nine doors change: some lose their ornaments, four change the way they open, two become breakable by fireball and axe, and one stops being breakable.
  • Level 3's spinners are switched on. Two teleporters that turn the party on the spot are inactive on the first disk.
  • Level 3's blue button stays pressed. The button that opens a door and works a nearby teleporter becomes a one-shot button that shows as pushed in once used.
  • A pocket for lost items. On level 4, a floor square no one can reach, behind a teleporter, becomes an invisible teleporter for items only. It sends them up to level 3, next to where the visible teleporter lands the party.

1.1, the rebalance11 December 1987 → 7 January 1988

This is the only release that changes how the game plays. Most of the changes are in the tables at the end of graphics.dat, where the numbers behind combat and spells live.

The Staff of Manar looks like a staff

Since 1.0 the Staff of Manar had been drawn with Fury's picture, and after the 11 December fix that picture is a sword. In 1.1 the staff uses the plain staff picture, the same as the Stick, the Teowand and the Yew Staff. The ANTIVEN POTION becomes the UNVEN POTION.

Items

  • The Lock Picks are gone. Alexander's mirror gets a Sling in their place, and the second set, in an alcove on level 7, is removed.
  • Two Rocks on level 2, to go with the Sling.
  • One Eye of Time fewer. The one on level 8 is removed.
  • Two FUL bombs on level 8 had a power of 0. They now have 120.

Level 6: a skeleton ambush

1.1 adds a whole trap. Two floor plates open two new false walls onto two new closets, each holding a group of skeletons. Two more groups wait nearby: 11 skeletons in all. A door that could only be opened from one side gets a second switch, on the far side.

Other fixes

  • Level 4's doors get tougher. Eight doors that a fireball or an axe could break no longer can, and two others become breakable.
  • Level 8's big switch. When pressed, it used to turn into a pushed-in green button. Now it shows the big switch pushed in.
  • Level 12: a false wall becomes a door with a button.
  • Level 9's riddle, "WHEN IS A ROCK NOT A ROCK", loses its two As.

1.2, the last touches7 January 1988 → 1 March 1988

  • The dungeon changes by four bits. The Sling and the three Rocks that 1.1 added are flagged important, like the champions' starting kit.
  • WAR CRY trains Parry, a Fighter skill, where it trained Influence, a Priest skill.
  • Two potions are renamed: UNVEN becomes BRO, and MON becomes MA.

The translationsGerman 1.2, 1 March 1988 · French 1.3, 20 May 1988

Both are the English 1.2 translated, and nothing else. Every record in the dungeon except the texts is identical, and so is every table. What changes is the words: the scrolls, the wall writing, the object and attack names, and the pictures with words drawn in them: the title, the entrance's ENTER and RESUME, the end, the save panel, FOOD and WATER, the resurrection screen.

  • The French disk extends the font. The texts carry no accents, but French needs the apostrophe, so 1.3 adds it to both fonts. The German disk spells umlauts as two letters (ZURUECK) and needs nothing new.
  • The translators started from older names. Where English 1.2 says BRO POTION and MA POTION, the German disk says GEGENGIFT (antidote) and MON TRANK, and the French POTION UNVEN and POTION MAN.
  • Wu Tse loses a title in German. In English Wu Tse is SON OF HEAVEN.
  • The rune names drift in French scrolls: YAP for YA, DOS for DES, OH EV SAR for OH EW SAR.

Where CSBWin's files come from

CSBWin plays 1.1's dungeon with the first 1.0's tables

The graphics.dat that ships with CSBWin's Dungeon Master is the 8 December 1987 disk's, byte for byte, except for its credits scroll, which is corrupted from "Dennis Walker" down ("Jaros" reads "Jeros"). Every original disk's credits are intact, so the damage came with a copy. A cracked copy of that disk in the same collection has exactly this file.

Its dungeon.dat, though, is 1.1's. So CSBWin's Dungeon Master has 1.1's Sling, Rocks and skeleton ambush, with 1.0's stamina costs, 1.0's Dragon, the jumping crack, Fury drawn without its blade, the Staff of Manar drawn as Fury, the ANTIVEN potion, and WAR CRY training Influence.
